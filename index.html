The three-step method

Before buying anything new

  1. 1

    Look

    What is actually wrong? Is it unsafe, worn, loose, stained or merely unfashionable?

  2. 2

    Use

    Check the cupboard, scrap basket and tool box. Borrow before buying.

  3. 3

    Mend

    Choose the smallest sound repair, then put the item back in service.
