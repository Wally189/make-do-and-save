The household workshop

Home DIY

Small, safe repairs that keep the home useful, dignified and welcoming. Know the limit of a job: gas, major electrical work, structural work and asbestos require competent specialists.

Start with maintenance

Beginner30 minutes

Silence a sticking door

Check hinges, tighten screws, clean painted edges and use a little suitable lubricant. Plane timber only after identifying the actual rubbing point.

Seasonal

Make a draught stopper

Use a worn cotton leg, fabric scraps and clean textile filling. Keep it removable so the threshold can be inspected and cleaned.

Repair

Refresh a loose drawer

Empty it, tighten hardware, wax wooden runners sparingly and replace only the failed component.

Routine

The ten-minute home round

Look for leaks, condensation, loose handles, damaged flexes and blocked vents. Record anything that needs qualified attention.

The safe order

  1. Stop and isolate hazards.
  2. Read the manufacturer’s guidance.
  3. Use the correct tool and protection.
  4. Do the smallest reversible repair.
  5. Test, tidy and record.
Never improvise
with gas, mains electricity, fire safety or suspected asbestos.
