The domestic church

Catholic Home

A household rhythm in which prayer gives purpose to work, economy makes room for generosity, and ordinary tasks become acts of love.

Before beginning work

Lord, receive the work of my hands. Give me patience to do what is needed, prudence to know my limits, gratitude for what I have, and charity toward those I serve. Through Christ our Lord. Amen.

A modest weekly rule

Sunday — receive and rest

Mass, a shared meal and genuine rest. Avoid turning domestic productivity into another master.

Monday — order

Review meals, essential repairs and appointments. Choose no more than three household priorities.

Wednesday — make

Give a short, protected period to a useful craft or repair. Pray for the eventual recipient.

Friday — simplify

Keep the meal meat-free, mend rather than shop, and direct some saved money or goods toward mercy.

Saturday — prepare

Tidy the principal living space, prepare Sunday clothing and food, and stop at enough.

Household patrons

The Holy Family
For work, family life and hidden holiness.

St Joseph the Worker
For craftsmanship, provision and quiet faithfulness.

St Martha
For hospitality ordered toward Christ.

St Francis of Assisi
For simplicity and gratitude for creation.

Discernment

Thrift is not hoarding. Simplicity is not neglect. Work is not worth measured only by output. The person comes before the project.
