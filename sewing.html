The sewing room

Make Do & Mend

Practical repairs, visible mending and thoughtful alterations that keep good clothes in service.

The repair basket

10 minutes

Sew on a button securely

Match the thread, reinforce the fabric if thin, leave a thread shank for heavy cloth, and finish the knot on the reverse.

Visible mend

Patch worn knees

Trim loose fibres, place a rounded patch behind or above the wear, then stitch around the edge without pulling the fabric out of shape.

Essential

Darn a small hole

Stabilise the area in a hoop, bridge the gap with parallel threads, then weave across them. Do not over-tighten.

Refashion

Turn a shirt into an apron

Reuse the button front, cut away damaged sleeves and collar, bind raw edges and add ties from sound offcuts.

Keep ready

  • Sharps and embroidery needles
  • Black, white and neutral thread
  • Small scissors and seam ripper
  • Safety pins and thimble
  • Saved buttons and sound scraps

Wash garments before long-term storage. Label repair pieces so unfinished work does not become clutter.
