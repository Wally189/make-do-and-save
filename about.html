ABOUT THE EXPERIMENT

I'M THE EXPERIMENT, NOT THE EXPERT.

One ordinary household trying to get financially healthier and live better by cooking properly, wasting less, repairing things and buying deliberately.

The public version

The author is a man in his mid-forties living in a modest house in Bristol. He has accumulated roughly £25,000 in debt and has been through a turbulent few years. A serious health event changed his perspective; a freer period followed; work and finances became difficult; things are now stabilising.

This site records the attempt to put household and finances back in order without turning life into punishment.

Faith, quietly present

Faith is part of the household background and may naturally appear in fasting, abstinence, Sunday, gratitude, restraint and purpose. It is not a condition of entry: almost everything practical here should remain useful to a reader who is not religious.

Privacy

Exact address, neighbourhood, employer, job title, creditor identities, account details and medical records are deliberately outside the public story.
