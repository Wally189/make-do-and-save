Needles at the ready

Knitting

Warm, practical pieces, careful repairs and small projects sized to the wool and time already available.

Useful patterns

Beginner

Garter-stitch washcloth

Cast on a practical square, knit every row, add a hanging loop and use cotton yarn suitable for repeated washing.

Scrap yarn

Striped household hot-water-bottle cover

Work a firm fabric from compatible wool remnants. Check the finished cover does not obstruct the stopper.

Repair

Swiss-darn a thinning heel

Duplicate the original stitches before a hole forms. Match fibre and weight where possible and keep the repair flexible.

Gift

Simple ribbed hat

Measure first, swatch honestly and choose a washable yarn. A folded brim gives extra warmth and tolerates growth.

The enough rule

Choose one active project and one repair. Finish or consciously retire them before beginning more.

Care

  • Wash according to fibre.
  • Dry flat when required.
  • Store clean and dry.
  • Repair moth damage promptly.
