The scrap basket

Crochet

Hard-wearing household pieces, useful gifts and small projects that give leftover yarn a proper purpose.

Practical makes

Cotton

Textured dishcloth

Use a dense stitch in washable cotton, finish firmly and make a pair so one can dry while the other is in use.

Scraps

Striped market bag

Join compatible yarns into a strong mesh body with reinforced handles. Test it with weight before taking it out.

Home

Rag-yarn basket

Cut worn cotton fabric into even strips and crochet a firm container for pegs, mending or pantry cloths.

Gift

Simple prayer-book cover

Measure the closed book, work a close fabric and add internal flaps without straining the binding.

Use scraps wisely

Sort by fibre, weight and washability before combining yarn. Very short remnants can become ties, markers or stuffing only when safe and suitable.

Avoid loose decorative loops on items intended for babies, pets or food preparation.
