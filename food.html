The household table

Food Plan

A pescatarian, whole-food pattern inspired by Daniel Plan principles: abundant plants, beans, whole grains, nuts and seeds; fish in moderation; limited dairy and highly processed food.

Health note: This is general meal-planning inspiration, not medical or dietary treatment. Allergies, pregnancy, kidney conditions, diabetes, eating disorders and other needs require individual professional advice.

Pantry foundations

Plants first

Build half the plate from vegetables or salad, then add whole grains and protein.

Affordable protein

Rotate lentils, beans, chickpeas, peas, eggs if used, tinned fish and modest portions of fresh or frozen fish.

Limited dairy

Treat cheese and cream as accents rather than the centre of a meal. Use unsweetened fortified alternatives where appropriate.

Friday practice

Keep Friday meals deliberately simple and meat-free, joining household economy to prayer, penance and generosity.

Waste less

  • Plan from what is already open.
  • Freeze labelled portions promptly.
  • Keep a “use first” fridge shelf.
  • Compost unavoidable plant waste.
